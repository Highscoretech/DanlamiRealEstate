# Open questions for the client

Raised after reviewing the material sent on 8 September 2026. Ordered by what blocks
work soonest.

## Blocking — needed before launch

1. **Transparent logo files.** Everything we have is a JPEG with a white box baked in, so
   the logo cannot sit on a dark header, a photograph, or the footer. We need SVG, or PNG
   with a transparent background, from whoever designed it.

2. **Is it "Danlami" or "Dan Lami"?** The logo reads **DANLAMI** as one word. The copy
   document says **Dan Lami Real Estate** throughout, including in the legal footer line
   "© 2026 Dan Lami Real Estate Ltd." The domain is `danlamirealestate.com`. We have to
   pick one and use it everywhere.

3. **Which domain is the real one?** We hold `danlamirealestate.com`, but their existing
   flyers and Instagram posts print `www.danlamirealestate.ng`. If `.ng` is already live
   or already advertised, we need to know — either we point one at the other, or we are
   about to split their traffic.

4. **Property photographs.** Everything received so far is screenshots of Instagram, not
   the original photos. They are 500–600px wide and heavily compressed — unusable at full
   width on a website. We need the original camera files for every property that goes on
   the site.

5. **Sections 10, 11 and 12 of the copy document were never sent.** The screenshots jump
   from 9 (Development Partnerships) to 13 (For Investors). Section 11 appears to be
   "Track Record" but we only have part of it.

6. **The closing note is cut off.** The client wrote "I would not put every number you have
   ever mentioned into the website yet. Things like number of agents trained, total
   clients, total transactions, years of experience — those should be…" and the screenshot
   ends. We have followed the instruction and left all statistics out. Please confirm.

7. **"Selected Projects" is still a placeholder.** In the track record section the client
   left four blank `[PROJECT NAME] / Location / Role` entries. These need filling in.

## Needed to build the forms

8. **The Google account that will own the spreadsheet.** Form submissions write into a
   Google Sheet. Whoever owns that account owns the data — it should be a company account,
   not a staff member's personal Gmail.

9. **Which email address receives form alerts.** Currently the only address we have is
   `danlamirealestate@gmail.com`, taken off an Instagram caption. Is that the right one, or
   should it be an address on the domain?

10. **What the partner registration form should collect.** The copy document does not
    mention partners at all, but the meeting did, and section 17 has a "Partner With Us"
    button. We need to know who counts as a partner and which fields to collect. Note that
    asking for NIN, ID uploads or bank details makes a privacy policy legally required
    under the Nigeria Data Protection Act.

## Needed for content

12. **Is the portrait Danlami Ojo?** We received one studio portrait (`assets/team/`) and

13. **Property details.** We can read prices and locations off the Instagram grid, but not

14. **Are the two Instagram accounts both live?** Captions reference both

15. **Is gold part of the brand?** The logo is teal and black, but the Garett Court flyers

    one development.

16. **Registration details.** The footer claims "Dan Lami Real Estate Ltd" — we need the RC
