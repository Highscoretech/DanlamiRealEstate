## The most important change

> Danlami, I would actually change the **architecture of the website**, not
> merely rewrite the old copy.

Your old website effectively told this story:

**Dan Lami → Who we are → What we do → Why we're good → Properties → Contact us.**

The new website should tell:

**YOU → WHAT YOU'RE BUILDING → WHAT STANDS IN YOUR WAY → OUR PHILOSOPHY → HOW WE
GUIDE YOU → THE RIGHT ASSET → OWNERSHIP → LEGACY.**

That's your six-part brand narrative embedded into the website without literally
telling visitors, *"Here is our hero, villain and guide."*

And there's one phrase from this exercise that I think could become almost as
important to your brand as **"Structured for Wealth. Built for Legacy."**

### DON'T JUST BUY PROPERTY. OWN ASSETS THAT MAKE SENSE.

That can become a recurring Dan Lami philosophy across the website, your videos,
presentations, investor meetings, prospectuses and campaigns.

- **"Africa's Luxury Real Estate Authority"** tells people **who you are**.
- **"Structured for Wealth. Built for Legacy."** tells them **what you believe**.
- **"Own assets that make sense."** tells the customer **what to do differently**.

Those three together give the rebrand a very coherent language.
